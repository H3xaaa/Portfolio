About

Hi, I'm Jonard Avenido, a game developer and graphic designer with a passion for building complete, polished experiences.

I don't just work on the visuals. I architect the systems too, from Firebase databases and user authentication to stats tracking and leaderboards. I've shipped real games and seen them through from concept to code.

During my OJT at Team Motorero Sportswear, I designed jerseys, sublimation tees, and polo shirts for real clients, working with Photoshop to produce print-ready apparel graphics.

I studied BSEMC (Bachelor of Science in Entertainment and Multimedia Computing) at University of Caloocan City from 2022 and graduated on April 27, 2026. The program focused on Graphic Design, Game Development, UI/UX, and backend systems, and many of the projects shown here were completed during that time.
